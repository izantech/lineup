# Next Steps

Now that you've seen the pipeline in action, here's where to go based on what you want to do next.

## Learn how things work

- **Want to understand the full pipeline?** Start with [The Pipeline](/concepts/pipeline) for a stage-by-stage breakdown of what happens and why.
- **Curious about the agents?** [Agents](/concepts/agents) explains each role, what tools they have, and why they use different models.
- **What are skills and how do they work?** [Skills](/concepts/skills) covers the slash commands that Lineup provides.
- **What are those YAML documents agents pass around?** [Ephemeral Documents](/concepts/ephemeral-documents) explains the structured data flow between stages.

## Customize the workflow

- **Want to change agent models or tools?** The [Customize Agents](/guides/customize-agents) guide walks through `/lineup:configure`.
- **Want to skip stages for simple tasks?** [Pipeline Tiers](/concepts/pipeline-tiers) explains when to use Full, Lightweight, or Direct mode.
- **Want to choose the right tier for your task?** The [Choose a Pipeline Tier](/guides/choose-tier) guide has a decision tree.

## Create reusable workflows

- **Want to define custom workflows for your project?** [Tactics](/concepts/tactics) explains what tactics are and how they work.
- **Want to build your own tactic?** The [Create a Tactic](/guides/create-tactic) guide has step-by-step instructions.
- **Want to see what ships with Lineup?** [Built-in Tactics](/concepts/built-in-tactics) covers the tactics included in the plugin.

## Get a codebase explanation

- **Want to understand unfamiliar code?** The [Use Explain](/guides/use-explain) guide shows how to use `/lineup:explain` to get structured explanations of any project component.

## Explore examples

- **Feature development** -- See a [full feature workflow](/examples/feature-development) from research to documentation.
- **Bug triage** -- See how Lineup handles [investigating and fixing bugs](/examples/bug-triage).
- **Documentation generation** -- See how to [generate missing docs](/examples/documentation-generation) for an existing codebase.
